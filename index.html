Rules and status labels

1D (70%) buys when a coin's daily close beats its previous 40-day high close while above its 200-day average. Stop 3 ATR, trailing stop 4 ATR on daily closes. Retries hourly that day; skips if price is more than 1.5% from the signal close. Up to 4 open at once (launch cap).

1H (30%) buys when the hourly close beats the previous 96-hour high close, with every trend check on: daily at/above its 200-day (or hourly 50-EMA over 200-EMA), daily above its 50-day, last 4-hour close above its 50-bar average. Stop 6 ATR, trailing 12 ATR. One try, next hour. Up to 2 open at once (launch cap).

The 70/30 money split is set once at launch and never rebalanced. Coins: this month's top-10 list. The old bot's rules are in Old bot at the bottom.

How to read this

bot2's entry rules checked on this month's top-10 coins, using the last closed candle, the same way the bot does. Each dot is one rule: met, not yet. A coin only fires when every dot on its side is filled.

Gap = how far the price still is below the breakout level (40-day high close for 1D, 96-hour high close for 1H). Tap a coin for each rule in words. 1D checks once a day at the daily close (10 am Sydney, 11 am during daylight saving) and retries hourly that day; 1H checks every hour, one try.

Once every other rule is met, a last check appears: the bot skips if price is more than 1.5% from the signal close. bot2 also skips a coin it already holds (holdings aren't shown on this public page). The old bot is manage-only, so its old rules aren't checked here.

What is money rotation?

Which crypto sectors money is moving into or out of: each sector's price vs BTC today and over 7 days, and its share of trading volume now vs its 7-day average. Same numbers as the Discord report. Not a trade signal.

It updates at 7am, 10am, 2pm and 6pm Sydney. The TradingView list (when there is one) holds coins in Money-IN sectors that haven't moved yet - a watchlist, not a signal.
