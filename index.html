How to read this page

Signals shows what is ready to fire on the last closed candle and what is closest, judged exactly as the bot judges it. Coins the bot already holds are marked HELD. "Show all" lists every check.

Bot health checks every bot trade against its rule: entry within 1.5% of the signal, risk within the setting, a stop on the exchange. R = result in multiples of the risk taken.

Realised is money banked from closed trades minus fees. Open trades are in Positions, not here.

Others dominance is every coin outside the top 10 as a share of the whole market (TradingView's OTHERS.D, from CoinGecko, offset −0.5 to match). Mike's rule: sell out of alts at 13%.

Breadth is the share of the 1D list above its 200-day. Above 90% has been the weak zone for new breakouts.

Read-only. Hyperliquid, CoinGecko and GMX public data, refreshed every 10 minutes.
